Privacy & Security
Your Data Stays on Your Computer

BLEETrader is designed with security as the foundation — your brokerage credentials never leave your machine.

🔒

Encrypted token storage — Your Schwab OAuth token is encrypted at rest with a passphrase you choose. It's stored locally in src/.env and never transmitted anywhere.

🏠

Runs locally only — The app binds to 127.0.0.1 and is not accessible from outside your network. Nothing is hosted or cloud-based.

🔑

OAuth only, no passwords — BLEETrader uses Schwab's official OAuth flow. Your Schwab password is never entered into or stored by the app.

✅

BLEE login verified — Your BLEE Quant account is verified via Firebase on every launch. Only active Pro/Ultimate subscribers can access trading features.
