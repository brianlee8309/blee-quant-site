Your exclusive trading tool

BLEETrader — Desktop Auto-Trade App

BLEETrader is a Windows desktop application that connects directly to your Charles Schwab brokerage account via the official Schwab API. Every trading day at 3:52 PM ET, it automatically pulls the latest BLEE allocation and executes the rebalance trades in your account.

As an Ultimate subscriber, BLEETrader gives you access to both investment models in the dropdown — choose which one to follow or switch any time. You remain in full control: BLEETrader shows a trade preview before executing, and you can override or cancel any trade.
