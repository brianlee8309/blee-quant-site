Daily Signal Page

bleeanalytics.com/index2.html  ·  Updated daily at 3:51 PM ET

The Daily Signal page is the core of your Starter subscription. Every trading day at 3:51 PM Eastern, the BLEE algorithm publishes today's ETF allocation — showing you exactly which funds to hold and at what percentage weight.

Here's what you'll find on the page and what to do with it:

  1. 1
    Today's Allocation — A donut chart and table showing the current ETF mix (e.g. SGOV 82%, GLDM 9%, TECL 8%). This is the model's recommended portfolio for the current market environment.
  2. 2
    Reallocation Status — The most important section. It compares today's allocation to yesterday's and tells you clearly: "No reallocation needed" (hold) or "Reallocation recommended" (trades required). Check this first every day after 3:51 PM ET.
  3. 3
    Investment Calculator — Enter your total portfolio value and the calculator converts the percentage weights into exact dollar amounts for each ETF. Use this to size your trades precisely.
  4. 4
    Historical Weight Changes — A chart showing how each ETF's allocation has shifted over the past 2 weeks. Useful for understanding recent model activity and trends.
💡 Daily routine: Check the page after 3:51 PM ET each trading day. If reallocation is recommended, log into your brokerage, sell holdings that decreased, then buy those that increased — proportionally to your portfolio size using the Investment Calculator.

Market Forecast Page

bleeanalytics.com/marketDailySummary.html  ·  Updated each morning

The Market Forecast page gives you a structured, data-driven snapshot of current market conditions — generated each morning by our Quant Analytics Research Engine. Think of it as your morning briefing before the trading day begins.

What it covers:

  1. 1
    Market Weather Score — A composite score (like a weather forecast) summarizing whether current conditions favor risk-on or risk-off positioning. Ranges from stormy to clear.
  2. 2
    Key Indicators — A breakdown of the signals feeding into the score: volatility (VIX), momentum (RSI), trend (moving averages), and macro environment. Each is rated and explained in plain language.
  3. 3
    Today's Outlook — A narrative summary written by the engine describing what the current data means for the day ahead — useful context for understanding why the allocation may change.
  4. 4
    Historical Forecast Archive — Browse past forecasts to see how market conditions have shifted over time and how the model responded.
💡 How to use it: Read the Market Forecast each morning for context, then check the Daily Signal after 3:51 PM ET to see if the allocation changed. The forecast helps you understand why the model is doing what it's doing — so you can follow the signal with confidence, not anxiety.
