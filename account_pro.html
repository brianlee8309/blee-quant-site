BLEETrader — Desktop Auto-Trade App

BLEETrader is a Windows desktop application that connects directly to your Charles Schwab brokerage account via the official Schwab API. Every trading day at 3:52 PM ET, it automatically pulls the latest BLEE allocation and executes the rebalance trades in your account — so you never have to manually log in and trade again.

You remain in full control: BLEETrader shows you a trade preview before executing, and you can override or cancel any trade at any time. No third parties hold your money. Your Schwab credentials stay in your account — BLEETrader uses OAuth, not your password.

Daily Signal Page

bleeanalytics.com/index2.html · Updated daily at 3:51 PM ET

If BLEETrader is running, it will handle the daily rebalance automatically. But you can always check the Daily Signal page to see today's allocation, monitor what the algorithm is doing, or verify trades manually.

  1. 1
    Today's Allocation — The current ETF mix (e.g. SGOV 82%, GLDM 9%, TECL 8%). This is what BLEETrader will trade to if rebalancing is needed today.
  2. 2
    Reallocation Status — Shows whether a rebalance is needed today. BLEETrader checks this automatically, but you can also verify here.
  3. 3
    Investment Calculator — If you want to rebalance manually instead, enter your portfolio value to get exact dollar amounts per ETF.
  4. 4
    Historical Weight Changes — A chart showing how each ETF's allocation has shifted over the past 2 weeks. Useful for understanding recent model behavior.
